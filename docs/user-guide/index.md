# LeaveMaestro User Guide

Use this guide to learn how to use LeaveMaestro as an employee, manager/leave approver, HR user, or tenant administrator. Instructions are organized around the task you want to complete rather than backend or technical concepts.

## Start here

- [Getting Started](getting-started/index.md) — sign in, activate a new account, use Google/GitHub sign-in, and understand role-based navigation.
- [Employees](employee/index.md) — view your staff information and entitlements, apply for leave, understand date rules, review requests and use the leave calendar.
- [Managers and Leave Approvers](manager/index.md) — review assigned requests, approve/reject leave and cancellations, and understand team visibility.
- [HR](hr/index.md) — create staff, review generated entitlements, maintain staff information and configure leave approvers.
- [Tenant Administrators](admin/index.md) — manage tenant-level leave types, calendars, policies, eligibility, approvers, roles and staff administration.
- [Account & Security](account-security/index.md) — account activation, password login, Google/GitHub account linking and logout.
- [Troubleshooting and FAQ](troubleshooting/index.md) — recover from common sign-in, permission, entitlement and leave-request problems.

## A note about configuration

LeaveMaestro is policy- and permission-driven. The leave types, balances, pages and actions available to you can differ by tenant, jurisdiction, employment details and assigned role. The guides describe the current product behavior while calling out areas that depend on configuration.

## Hosted evaluation environment

The project-hosted environment is for temporary testing/evaluation. Use fictional or non-production data only and do not enter real employee or personal data.
